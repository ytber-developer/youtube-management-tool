## Title
What Happens to You in Space Without a Spacesuit?

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Movies say you'd explode or freeze solid in seconds. The real answer, pieced together from NASA research, old animal studies and a few frightening accidents, is stranger. Second by second, we follow what would happen to your lungs, blood, mouth and skin, why your saliva would boil while your mouth goes ice cold at the same time, how long you would stay awake, how long rescuers would have, which famous movie got it right, and the skintight spacesuit of the future that grew out of all this.
```
