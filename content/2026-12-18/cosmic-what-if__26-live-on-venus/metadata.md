## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Venus is almost exactly Earth's size, yet its surface would crush you, cook you, and poison you within moments. Soviet landers survived barely two hours. But thirty miles up, NASA scientists found what may be the most Earth-like place in the solar system, where ordinary air floats like helium. We explore both, plus where Venus's water went.
```
