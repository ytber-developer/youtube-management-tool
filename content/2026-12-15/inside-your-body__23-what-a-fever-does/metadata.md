A fever is not your body breaking down. It is a deliberate, controlled reset of the thermostat in your brain. Follow the battle plan step by step: how immune signals raise your set point, why you shiver and feel cold while burning up, why lizards and honeybees run fevers too, what the extra heat does to germs and immune cells, and when a fever means it is time to call a doctor.
