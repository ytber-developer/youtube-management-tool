## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Your heart beats over one hundred thousand times a day and pumps more than two thousand gallons of blood, and every beat starts with a tiny electrical spark inside the heart itself. Here is how the two pumps, four valves and built-in pacemaker work together, and what nuclear bomb tests revealed about whether your heart cells ever get replaced.
```
