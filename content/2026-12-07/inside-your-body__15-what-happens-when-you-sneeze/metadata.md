## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
A sneeze is over in an instant, but inside you it is a precise, six-step reflex: a nerve alarm in your nose, a decision in your brainstem, a giant breath, a sealed throat and a blast of air that can carry up to forty thousand droplets. We slow it down step by step, test common sneeze myths, and explain why you should never hold one in.
```
