## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Pluto is a tiny, far-away world, yet when New Horizons flew past in 2015 it showed glaciers of nitrogen ice, mountains made of water ice, a very thin sky, and a giant moon that never moves. We imagine standing there: the temperature, the gravity, the long day, and the strange slow motion of the ground itself.
```
