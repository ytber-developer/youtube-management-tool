## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Give Earth a ring system like Saturn's and the sky turns spectacular, but rings also cast shadows, threaten satellites and slowly rain debris. We tour the view from the equator to the poles, explain how planets get rings in the first place, and look at new evidence that Earth may have worn a ring about 466 million years ago.
```
