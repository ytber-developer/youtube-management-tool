For nearly seventy years, scientists argued about what makes that pop in your knuckles. We follow the whole story: the slippery fluid inside your joints, the X-ray studies of 1947 and 1971, the MRI scan that finally filmed a knuckle cracking, and the 2018 math model that suggests both sides were partly right. Then we test the arthritis myth, from one doctor's sixty-year experiment on his own hands to larger studies, and cover when a cracking joint is worth a visit to the doctor.
