## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
What actually happens when you stub your toe? Follow a pain signal from nociceptors in your skin, up the spinal cord, into the brain, and back down again. Learn the gate control idea behind why rubbing helps, why pain works as a protective alarm, what referred pain is, and when pain deserves a call to a doctor or emergency services.
```
