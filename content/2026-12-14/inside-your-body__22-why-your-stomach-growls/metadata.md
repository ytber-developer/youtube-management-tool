## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
That loud growl in a quiet room has a name: borborygmi. Follow the clues to find out what really makes the noise, why your gut runs a cleaning wave about every ninety to one hundred twenty minutes when you have not eaten, how the hormone motilin links that wave to hunger, and why doctors worry more about a silent belly than a noisy one.
```
