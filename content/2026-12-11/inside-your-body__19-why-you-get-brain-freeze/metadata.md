## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Why does cold ice cream make your forehead hurt? We walk through what headache experts know about brain freeze, a 2012 lab study that watched an artery in the brain widen at the exact moment the pain began, and a 2002 classroom trial that found eating ice cream fast roughly doubled the chances of a brain freeze. Plus why the pain shows up in your forehead, how to stop it in seconds, and when a headache is worth a doctor's visit.
```
