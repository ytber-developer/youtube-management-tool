## Description (copy nguyên khối)

Follow a single breath from your nose down a branching tree of airways to hundreds of millions of tiny air sacs, see how oxygen and carbon dioxide trade places, how your diaphragm pulls air in, why breathing runs on autopilot, and roughly how much air you move in a day. Plus the breathing signs that mean it is time to see a doctor.
