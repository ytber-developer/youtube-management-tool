## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Gamma-ray bursts are the brightest explosions we know. What if one went off close to home, with its jet aimed at Earth? We explain what causes a burst, how it could strip the ozone layer, why scientists once suggested a burst helped cause the Ordovician extinction, why that idea is unproven, and how rare the real danger is.
```
