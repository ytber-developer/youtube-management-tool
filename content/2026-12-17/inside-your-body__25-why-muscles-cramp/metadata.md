## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
A muscle cramp can turn your calf into a rock-hard knot in seconds. For years the blame went to dehydration and low salt, but a growing body of research points to something else: tired muscles whose nerve signals get stuck in the on position. Here is how a normal muscle squeeze works, what goes wrong in a cramp, what a famous pickle juice experiment revealed, and what commonly helps.
```
