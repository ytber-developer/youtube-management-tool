Spend one hour near a giant black hole, and gravity can slow your clock so much that years pass back home. Using real relativity, from Pound-Rebka and GPS to Kip Thorne's Gargantua in Interstellar, we work out how time dilation really works, what it would feel like, how much time passes far away, and where the movie stretches the science.
