## Title
What If Earth Had Two Suns? Life on a Real Tatooine

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Many Sun-like stars have a partner. So what if ours did? We try it two ways: a Tatooine Earth circling a pair of stars, with double shadows, double sunsets and eclipses of one sun by the other, and an Earth with a distant second sun that turns night into twilight. Along the way: real two-sun planets found by Kepler and TESS, why you can't just add a second Sun, and the Sun's possible lost twin.
```
