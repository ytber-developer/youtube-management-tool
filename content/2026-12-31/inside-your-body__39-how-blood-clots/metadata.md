## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
A tiny cut stops bleeding in minutes, and you never have to think about it. Watch the three steps your body uses to seal a leak: blood vessels squeeze, platelets pile up into a plug, and a mesh of fibrin locks it in place. Learn what the clotting cascade is, why vitamin K matters, how your body keeps clots from spreading and then dissolves them, why clots are protective but can become risky in the wrong place, and the warning signs that call for medical help.
```
