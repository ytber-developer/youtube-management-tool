Snap your fingers and every molecule of air on Earth disappears. What happens to you in the first second, the first minute, and the first day? We follow the silence, the black sky, the boiling and freezing oceans, the deadly sunlight and the hail of space rock, then look at how Mars really did lose most of its air, and why Earth's thin blanket is safe for now.
