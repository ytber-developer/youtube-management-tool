## Title
What If Earth Froze Solid? The Snowball Earth Mystery

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Hundreds of millions of years ago, rocks suggest, ice may have reached the tropics. In this animated what-if we ask what it would be like if the whole Earth fell into a global deep freeze. We look at the evidence behind the Snowball Earth hypothesis, how the ice-albedo feedback could lock a planet in the cold, where life could have survived, and how volcanoes may have broken the freeze. We also cover the big scientific argument: some researchers prefer a milder slushball Earth with patches of open water. Much about this ancient climate remains debated.
```
