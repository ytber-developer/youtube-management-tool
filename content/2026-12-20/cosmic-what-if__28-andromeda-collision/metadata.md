## Title
What If the Milky Way and Andromeda Collided? Would You Survive?

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
The Andromeda galaxy is rushing toward us at about 250,000 miles per hour. We fast-forward billions of years to watch the crash: why almost no stars would collide, what happens to the Sun and to two supermassive black holes, and the giant galaxy that could form. Then the 2025 plot twist: new Hubble and Gaia data give the collision only about a coin-flip chance.
```
