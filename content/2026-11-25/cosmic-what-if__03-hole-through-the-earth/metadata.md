## Title
What If You Jumped Into a Hole Through the Earth?

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Dig a tunnel straight through the planet, jump in, and how long until you pop out the other side? We start at the deepest hole humans have ever drilled, travel through the crust, the caramel-like mantle and an iron core nearly as hot as the Sun, and find out why gravity gets stronger on the way down. Then the physics gets strange: the fall takes about thirty-eight minutes, matches half a satellite orbit, and works for almost any straight tunnel. Plus the inner core's mysterious spin, and where you would really come out (hint: not China).
```
