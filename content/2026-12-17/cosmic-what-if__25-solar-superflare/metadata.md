## Description (copy nguyên khối)

A superflare from the Sun would arrive in eight minutes, light up the sky with auroras, and then go after the power grid. We follow the first days hour by hour, from radio blackouts and satellite failures to months without electricity, and look at the 1859 Carrington Event, the 1989 Quebec blackout, the 2012 near miss, and new evidence that Sun-like stars blast out superflares about once a century.
