Hollywood says a Martian dust storm will knock you flat. NASA says the wind would feel like a breeze. So what is the real danger? We walk through a planet-wide storm from the inside: the darkness, the power crisis that ended the Opportunity rover, toxic dust, and the way these storms quietly drain water out of Mars.
