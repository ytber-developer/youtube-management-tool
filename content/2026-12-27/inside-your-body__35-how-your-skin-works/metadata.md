Your skin is your largest organ, and it quietly replaces itself about every month. Take a layer-by-layer tour of the epidermis, dermis, and hypodermis: how the waterproof top layer blocks germs, how about forty thousand skin cells are shed every day, how sweat and blood vessels control your temperature, how nerve endings sense touch, and how melanin works like built-in sunshade. Plus when a changing spot is worth showing to a doctor.
