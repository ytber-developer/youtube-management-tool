## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
In the movies, the asteroid belt is a storm of tumbling boulders. In real life, it is mostly empty space. Using real data from Pioneer 10, Dawn, and NASA and other sources, we fly a spaceship through the belt between Mars and Jupiter and look at how far apart the rocks really are, why all of them together weigh less than the Moon, what the giant Ceres is like, and why probes cross the belt without getting hit.
```
