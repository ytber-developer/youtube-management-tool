## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Your lungs hold enough oxygen for about four minutes, so why do most people give up after one? We follow a single breath hold stage by stage: the carbon dioxide alarm, the stubborn diaphragm, the reflex that slows your heart when your face hits water, the sea nomads with extra-large spleens, and the freediver who held on for twenty-nine minutes. Plus the one place where holding your breath is genuinely risky, and when to talk to a doctor.
```
