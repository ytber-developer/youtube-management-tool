## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Voyager 1 left Earth in 1977 and is now the farthest spacecraft humans have ever made. Using real NASA data, we ride along: past the Pale Blue Dot, through the heliopause into interstellar space, to a signal that now takes about a day to arrive. Along the way we look at the Golden Record, the fading power, and the long road to the next stars.
```
