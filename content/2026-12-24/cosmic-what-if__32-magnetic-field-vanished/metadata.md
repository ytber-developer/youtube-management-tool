## Title
What If Earth's Magnetic Field Disappeared Tomorrow?

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Earth's magnetic field is an invisible shield made by molten iron deep underground. What would happen if it switched off? Not what the disaster movies say. We follow the first second, the first day, the first years and the far future, and look at the time 42,000 years ago when the field nearly did vanish.
```
