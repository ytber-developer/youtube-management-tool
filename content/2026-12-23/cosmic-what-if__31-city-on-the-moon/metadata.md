## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Astronauts have flown around the Moon again, and space agencies are planning to stay. So what would it really take to turn a landing site into a city? Step by step, we pick a site, find water, make air out of moon dust, hide from radiation in a lava tube, and discover why your clocks would run fast.
```
