## Title
Why Reading in a Car Makes You Sick (and Drivers Don't Get It)

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Why does a book in the back seat leave you queasy while the driver feels fine? This episode follows one road trip to show how your eyes, inner ears, and body send reports to your brain, what happens when those reports disagree, why drivers rarely get carsick, how the brain can adapt, and simple tricks like watching the horizon and getting fresh air. Plus when it is worth talking to a doctor.
```
