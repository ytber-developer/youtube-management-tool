## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
If the Sun vanished right now, you wouldn't know for eight minutes, and neither would Earth's orbit, because gravity travels at the speed of light. We follow what happens next, minute by minute and then million year by million year: a black sky, a planet flung into interstellar space, vanishing winds, weaker tides, frozen oceans and falling air. Then we bust the suffocation myth, dive to the deep-sea vents and underground microbes where life could hang on, visit Iceland's volcanic heat, and meet the rogue planets and icy moons that already live this way.
```
