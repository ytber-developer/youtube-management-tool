## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Exercise is not only for your muscles. Follow one brisk walk to see what the research says about blood flow to the brain, a growth-supporting protein called BDNF, the hippocampus and memory, mood and the so-called runner's high (which scientists now think is more than just endorphins), plus what the CDC and the World Health Organization suggest for how much activity adults need. An honest look at what is solid and what is still uncertain.
```
