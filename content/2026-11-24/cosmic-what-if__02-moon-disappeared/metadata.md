## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
What would actually change if the Moon blinked out of existence tonight? Darker nights come first, then coral reefs and night hunters lose their clock, tides shrink to a third, spring tides and eclipses vanish, and Earth stops its monthly wobble. The slowest change is the biggest: the Moon steadies Earth's tilt and our seasons, and it has been stretching our days for billions of years. Then the twist: the Moon really is leaving, about one and a half inches every year.
```
