## Title
How Your Body Digests a Meal: The 30-Foot Journey

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Follow a single slice of pizza on its journey of about thirty feet through your digestive tract: saliva that starts digesting starch, a stomach that churns food into chyme, a small intestine about twenty-two feet long with an inner surface of about half a badminton court, and a large intestine full of helpful bacteria. Find out how long digestion really takes and why your gut has its own nervous system.
```
