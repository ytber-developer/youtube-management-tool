## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Your liver handles more than five hundred jobs, from storing sugar and making bile to building blood proteins and turning toxic ammonia into urea. Follow it through a single day, see why almost everything you absorb passes through it first, and learn how this remarkable organ can regrow after a large piece is removed.
```
