## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
One morning you wake up and everything weighs twice as much, including you. We live through the first day under double gravity, from getting out of bed and climbing stairs to your heart, your bones and a fall from a ladder, then zoom out to the air, airplanes, the tallest trees, the Moon, your calendar and what it would take to launch a rocket off a heavier Earth.
```
