## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Step outside on a freezing morning and your body launches a five-part defense plan: narrowing blood vessels, goosebumps, a hidden heater called brown fat, shivering that can multiply your heat output, and the feeling of cold that pushes you to grab a coat. Here is how each one works, why your fingers get cold first, the warning signs of hypothermia and frostbite, and whether cold weather really gives you a cold.
```
