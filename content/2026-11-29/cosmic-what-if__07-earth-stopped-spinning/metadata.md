## Title
What If Earth Stopped Spinning? Fast vs. Slow

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Earth carries you east at hundreds of miles per hour without you feeling a thing. What happens if it stops depends on how fast it stops: one version is over in minutes, the other redraws the map of the world, turns a day into a year and strands your satellites. We bust the 'flung into space' myth, visit Venus, the planet that almost did stop, and rewind to a time when a day lasted under ten hours. Then the twist: Earth's spin is changing right now, and your clocks have to keep up.
```
