## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Take one ordinary one-foot jump and repeat it on every planet in the solar system, plus the Moon, Pluto, the Sun and a comet. Where would you fly highest, where would you barely leave the ground, and where would you never come back down? A planet-by-planet tour of gravity, with a few surprises: why Mercury and Mars tie, why you would jump higher on giant Saturn than on Earth, and the comet where a single hop sends you into space forever.
```
