## Title
How Big Is the Universe, Really? Zooming Out Step by Step

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Zoom out from Earth, past the Moon, Voyager 1 and the nearest stars, across the Milky Way and the two trillion galaxies beyond it, all the way to the oldest light there is. Along the way: how Edwin Hubble discovered, only a century ago, that other galaxies exist at all, why the visible universe is 93 billion light-years wide even though it is only 13.8 billion years old, what scientists still can't say about what lies past the edge, and the twist that the universe is slowly hiding itself from us.
```
