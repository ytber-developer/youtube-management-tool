## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Rogue planets drift through the galaxy with no star of their own, and there may be more of them than stars. What would happen if one wandered through our solar system? We follow an imaginary dark intruder from the first faint dot in a telescope to the comet cloud, the giant planets and Earth's orbit, look at the real stars that have passed close to us, and ask what would become of Earth if it were flung out into the dark.
```
