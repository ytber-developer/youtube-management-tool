## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Proxima b is the closest known planet outside our solar system, an Earth-mass world in its star's habitable zone. We plan the trip, from Voyager speed to NASA's record-breaking Parker Solar Probe to laser-driven light sails, then imagine arriving under a red dwarf whose flares can make it thousands of times brighter in ultraviolet light.
```
