## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
The stuffy nose and sore throat of a cold are mostly your own immune system at work, not damage from the virus. Follow a cold from the first virus landing in your nose to the antibodies that finally win: how it spreads, why your cool nose suits it, when you're most contagious, and why you keep catching new ones. We also test common myths about cold weather, antibiotics and vitamin C, explain what actually helps, and say when a cold is worth a call to the doctor.
```
