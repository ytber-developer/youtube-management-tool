## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
You do it about nine times a day, you started before you were born, and you can catch it from a friend, or even your dog. We slow a yawn down step by step, test the oxygen and boredom myths, follow the evidence that yawns help cool your brain, and dig into why yawns are contagious, including a study that found empathy may not explain who catches them. Plus: when a lot of yawning is worth a chat with your doctor.
```
