## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Falling into the Sun sounds easy. It is actually one of the hardest trips in the solar system. We cheat, stop you dead in Earth's orbit, and let you fall: sixty-five days down, past the spot where NASA's Parker Solar Probe flew on Christmas Eve 2024, through a corona hotter than the surface, and on to a core at 27 million degrees Fahrenheit.
```
