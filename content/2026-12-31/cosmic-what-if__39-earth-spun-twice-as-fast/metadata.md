## Description (copy nguyên khối)

What if a day lasted only twelve hours? We look at what would change if Earth spun twice as fast: your weight at the equator, a bigger equatorial bulge, oceans shifting, stronger Coriolis effects on storms and winds, satellites that need new orbits, and why Earth's spin has actually been slowing for billions of years.
