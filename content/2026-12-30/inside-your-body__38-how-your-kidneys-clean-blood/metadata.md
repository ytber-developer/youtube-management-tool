## Title
How Your Kidneys Clean Your Blood: 180 Liters a Day

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Two fist-sized organs filter about one hundred eighty liters of fluid every day, and then take almost all of it back. Follow a drop of blood through a nephron to see how the glomerulus filters, how the tubules reclaim water, salt, and sugar, why only about one to two liters ends up as urine, and how your kidneys also make hormones that control blood pressure, red blood cells, and calcium. Plus when changes are worth mentioning to a doctor.
```
