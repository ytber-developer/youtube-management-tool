## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
The Big Bang is not an explosion you could watch from the outside, but scientists have pieced together a remarkably detailed timeline of the first second and the minutes after it. In this animated what-if, we follow the story from cosmic inflation to a sea of quarks and gluons, from ghostly neutrinos to the first atomic nuclei, and finally to the oldest light we can see, the cosmic microwave background. Along the way we flag what is well tested and what is still educated guesswork, because the earliest moments are the least certain.
```
