## Description (copy nguyên khối)

Beneath the frozen shell of Jupiter's moon Europa may lie an ocean with two to three times as much water as all of Earth's oceans. What if you dived in? We go through the ice, past deadly radiation and weak gravity, into a dark salty sea, ask whether hydrothermal vents could support life, and follow NASA's Europa Clipper on its way to find out.
