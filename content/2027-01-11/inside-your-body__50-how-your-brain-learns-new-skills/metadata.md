## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Practice a new skill and your brain physically changes. Follow a first guitar lesson to see how synapses strengthen with repetition, how sleep helps lock new memories in, why the insulation around nerve fibers called myelin may matter for smooth, fast movement, and why spreading your practice over days tends to beat cramming. Plus an honest look at what scientists are still working out.
```
