## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Light crosses a room faster than you can blink, so you never notice it moving. But what if it crawled along at the pace of a bicycle? Using the classic Mr Tompkins thought experiment by physicist George Gamow, we imagine a world where seeing is delayed, the Sun is a thousand light-years of waiting away, and relativity becomes everyday life. We also look at the real numbers: how fast light actually is, and why GPS satellites have to correct their clocks for relativity. This is a thought experiment, not a prediction: change the speed of light in the real universe and atoms, stars and people would not work at all.
```
