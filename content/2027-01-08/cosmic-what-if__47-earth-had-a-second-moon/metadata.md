## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Could Earth have two Moons? We look at the Hill sphere that limits where a moon can live, how tides would change, and the real objects that already follow Earth: quasi-moons like Kamo'oalewa and temporary mini-moons such as 2024 PT5.
```
