## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Blow Earth up to the size of Jupiter, rocks, oceans and all. Would it be a giant new home, or something much stranger? We work out what a Jupiter-sized rocky Earth would weigh, how hard it would pull on you, why it would probably turn into a gas giant anyway, why it still could not become a star, and why its people could never launch a chemical rocket into space.
```
