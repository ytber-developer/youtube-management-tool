## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Step into a dark movie theater and you are nearly blind, then slowly the room appears. Follow that moment to see how rods and cones divide the work, how the pigment rhodopsin is rebuilt, why full dark adaptation can take about twenty to thirty minutes, what your pupils really contribute, and why dim red light is used by people who want to keep their night vision. Plus when trouble seeing in dim light is worth an eye exam.
```
