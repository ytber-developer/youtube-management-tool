## Description (copy nguyên khối)

You have roughly one hundred thousand hairs on your scalp, and every one of them is on its own schedule. See how a hair follicle builds a hair, how the growth cycle moves from anagen to catagen to telogen, how fast hair grows, why losing about a hundred hairs a day is normal, and what is really happening inside the follicle when hair turns gray. Plus when thinning or sudden hair loss is worth mentioning to a doctor.
