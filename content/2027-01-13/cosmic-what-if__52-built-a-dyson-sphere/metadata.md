## Title

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
A Dyson sphere is one of the boldest ideas in science: surround a star with machines and capture its power. How much energy is that, how much material would you need, and why do scientists say a solid shell would never work? We follow the idea from Freeman Dyson's 1960 paper and the Kardashev scale to swarms of solar collectors, Mercury as a building site, and the strange dimming of Tabby's Star, which briefly made headlines as a possible alien megastructure. It is all speculative, and we say so where the science runs out.
```
