## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Follow one night of sleep from lights out to morning. Meet the stages of non-REM sleep and REM sleep, see why a sleep cycle lasts about ninety minutes and why you pass through four to six of them, learn how deep sleep may replay and file away memories, and look at a famous mouse study on brain cleaning (the glymphatic system) with honest caveats about what is still unknown. Plus how much sleep adults need and when to talk with a doctor about sleep trouble.
```
