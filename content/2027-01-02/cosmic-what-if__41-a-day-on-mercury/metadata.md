## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
A day on Mercury lasts about 176 Earth days, which is two Mercury years. The sunrise can stop and reverse, the afternoon heat reaches about 430 degrees Celsius, and the night drops to about minus 180. And yet there is ice hiding in the dark at the poles. We walk through one very long day on the closest planet to the Sun.
```
