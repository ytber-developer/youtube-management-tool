## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Follow a single sip of water through your body: the trick your mouth uses to switch off thirst early, how water reaches your blood within about five minutes, the jobs it does from cooling you down to cushioning your joints, and how your kidneys filter about one hundred fifty quarts of blood a day to keep the level just right. Plus how much you really need, what urine color tells you, and when dehydration needs a doctor.
```
