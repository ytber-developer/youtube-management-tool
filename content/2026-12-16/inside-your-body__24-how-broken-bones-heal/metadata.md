When you break a bone, a repair crew moves in within hours. Follow the four stages of fracture healing, from the first clot to the soft cartilage bridge, the hard callus and years of remodeling, and learn why casts matter, why kids heal faster, why pain fading does not mean a bone is healed, and why bone is one of the few tissues that heals without a scar.
