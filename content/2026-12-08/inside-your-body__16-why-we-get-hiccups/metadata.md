## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Hiccups are a sudden spasm of your diaphragm followed by a snap of your vocal cords, but why your body does it is still an open case. We look at the triggers, the reflex loop in your brainstem, and three scientific suspects: leftover gill-breathing wiring, a burping reflex for babies, and a brain-training signal in newborns. Plus which home cures have evidence, and when hiccups mean it is time to see a doctor.
```
