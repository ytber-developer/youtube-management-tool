## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Most laughter has nothing to do with jokes. Follow a single laugh through your body: the breathing bursts that make each ha, the brief workout for your heart, the feel-good opioids released in your brain, and why hearing laughter makes you want to join in. Plus what tickled apes and rats reveal about how old laughter really is.
```
