## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
A sunburn is not heat damage. It is your skin reacting to ultraviolet rays that injure the DNA inside its cells. Follow one sunburn hour by hour: the silent damage, the self-destructing sunburn cells, the redness that peaks a day later, the peeling on day three, and the DNA damage that stays after the skin looks healed. Plus what a tan really means, how to prevent a burn, and when to get medical help.
```
