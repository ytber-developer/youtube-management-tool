## Description (copy nguyên khối)

How does a vaccine actually work? Follow your immune system as it meets a new germ: the wanted-poster cells, helper T cells, antibody-making B cells, and the memory cells that make you faster the next time. Then see how vaccines rehearse that response safely, the main kinds of vaccines including mRNA, why protection takes a few weeks, and how it all began with cowpox in 1796.
