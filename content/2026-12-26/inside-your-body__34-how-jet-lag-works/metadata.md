## Title
How Jet Lag Scrambles Your Body Clock (East Is Worse)

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Fly from New York to Paris and your body lands six hours behind. Follow one trip to see how the master clock in your brain, about twenty thousand nerve cells that each keep time, sets the rhythm for nearly every organ; why that clock runs about eleven minutes longer than a day; and why that tiny difference makes flying east so much harder than flying west. Plus how to time light, caffeine and sleep to help your clock catch up.
```
