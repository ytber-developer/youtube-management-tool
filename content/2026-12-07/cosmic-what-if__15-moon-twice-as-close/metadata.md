## Title
What If the Moon Were Twice as Close to Earth?

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Move the Moon halfway to Earth and the night sky becomes spectacular, but the real drama is in the tides. We work out how much brighter the Moon would be, how short a month would get, why every solar eclipse would turn total, and how the ground itself would rise and fall. Then the twist: Earth really did have a much closer Moon, long ago, and it is still slipping away.
```
