## Title
What If the Sun Became a Black Hole?

## Description (copy nguyên khối)
```
Crush the Sun into a black hole of the same mass and Earth would not get sucked in. It would keep orbiting exactly as before, in total darkness. We follow the first eight minutes, the long freeze that comes after, what could survive, what you would see if you flew to the black hole, and why the real Sun will never become one.
```
