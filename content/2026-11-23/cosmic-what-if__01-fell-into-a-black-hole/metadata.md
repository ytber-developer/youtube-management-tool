What would actually happen if you fell into a black hole? We ride all the way down: why a small black hole would tear you apart before you reach the edge, why a giant like Sagittarius A* lets you cross in one piece, what NASA's supercomputer shows you would see on the three-hour fall, why your friends would watch you freeze, and how a near miss could leave you younger than your crew. Then the twist: black holes themselves may slowly disappear.
