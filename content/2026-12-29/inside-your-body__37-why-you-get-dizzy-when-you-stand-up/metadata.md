You jump off the couch and the room goes fuzzy for a moment. What just happened? Follow your blood as gravity pulls roughly seven hundred milliliters of it toward your legs, and see how pressure sensors called baroreceptors in your neck and chest trigger a lightning-fast fix by speeding your heart and tightening blood vessels. Plus why it happens more in some people, simple ways to stand up smoothly, and exactly when frequent dizziness is worth a call to your doctor.
